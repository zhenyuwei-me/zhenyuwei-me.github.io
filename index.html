Building whole-body skills for humanoids. From human motion to robot action.

My research spans whole-body dexterous manipulation, human-to-robot motion retargeting, and robot learning from egocentric and tactile data.

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Information Science and Technology, ShanghaiTech University, advised by Prof. Jingya Wang. As part of a joint doctoral training program, I am also advised by Dr. Baoxiong Jia at the Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI).

DexHLM: An Egocentric Dexterous Human Loco-Manipulation Dataset for Humanoids with Tactile Prediction

DexHLM pairs first-person RGB-D with body motion, hand motion, object trajectories, and tactile measurements. It uses touch to refine contact and predicts tactile signals from egocentric observations to support recovery of whole-body interactions.

Zhenyu Wei*, Haoyang Luo*, Chixuan Zhang, Guo Chen, Suting Ni, Siyuan Huang, Baoxiong Jia†, Jingya Wang†

† Corresponding authors

  • TactiDex: A Real-World Tactile-Guided Benchmark for Human-Like Dexterous Manipulation

    TactiDex combines whole-hand tactile measurements with hand motion and object states. Its TactiSkill framework uses these signals to transfer human demonstrations to single-hand and bimanual robot manipulation.
